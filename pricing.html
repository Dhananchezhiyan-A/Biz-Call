FAQ

Pricing Questions Answered

Is there a free trial available?
Yes — all plans come with a 14-day free trial with full enterprise feature access. No credit card required to start.
Can I change plans at any time?
Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
Are there any setup fees or hidden charges?
Zero setup fees. Zero hidden charges. The price you see is all-inclusive. Call usage beyond plan limits is billed transparently at per-minute rates shown in your dashboard.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, NEFT/RTGS bank transfers, and corporate purchase orders for Enterprise customers. GST invoices are generated automatically.
Does the per-agent pricing include all features?
Yes. Every agent seat on a plan includes full access to all features included in that tier. There are no per-feature unlocks — you get the complete platform.
How does the yearly discount work?
Choose annual billing and save 20% on every plan. You're billed for 12 months upfront, and the discounted amount is locked in for the full year — even if you add agents.
