FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Biz Call?
Biz Call is an enterprise cloud telephony and AI communications platform. We offer Cloud PBX, AI Voice Agents, Smart IVR, Power Dialer, WhatsApp API, Call Analytics, CRM Integration, and Team Chat — all in one unified platform.
How long does setup take?
Most businesses are live within 30 minutes for basic setups. Complex enterprise deployments with CRM integration and custom IVR flows typically take 2–4 hours with our onboarding team's guidance.
Can I port my existing phone numbers?
Yes. We support full number porting for local, mobile, and toll-free numbers across India and 50+ countries. The process typically takes 5–7 business days. During porting, your calls are automatically forwarded so there's zero downtime.
What languages does the AI Voice Agent support?
Our AI Voice Agent supports English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, and Assamese — plus major international languages including Arabic, Spanish, French, and German.
Is my call data secure?
Yes. All call recordings and data are encrypted using 256-bit AES at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Our infrastructure is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, with data residency options in India for regulated industries.
Do you integrate with Salesforce / HubSpot / Zoho?
Yes, we have native one-click integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Freshdesk, Pipedrive, Freshsales, LeadSquared, and 35+ more. For custom CRMs, we provide a full REST API and webhooks.
What is the SLA for uptime?
We guarantee 99.9% platform uptime with automatic failover across three data centres. Enterprise plans include a contractual SLA with compensation for any breach, plus a dedicated status page at status.bizcallcti.com.
Is there a mobile app for agents?
Yes. We offer native iOS and Android softphone apps for agents, plus a web browser-based softphone that requires no installation. All apps support full IVR controls, call recording, and CRM screen pops.
