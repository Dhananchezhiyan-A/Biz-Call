About Us

Reimagining How
Businesses Communicate

Biz Call Solutions was founded in 2018 by a team of enterprise telecom engineers and AI researchers who saw a fundamental mismatch: businesses were spending enormous sums on legacy telephony infrastructure that was holding them back, not propelling them forward.

Today, we serve 2,000+ businesses across India and Southeast Asia — from 10-agent startups to 2,000-seat enterprise contact centres. Our platform processes over 50 million calls per month with a 99.9% uptime SLA.

We build technology that's as intelligent as it is reliable. Every product we ship — from the AI Voice Agent to the real-time analytics engine — is designed to give businesses a genuine operational edge, not just check a compliance box.
